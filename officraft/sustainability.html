Methodology

How we measure.

A four-question rubric that every number on this page has been answered to. If your sustainability lead asks any of these, the answer is on the page.

1. What is the boundary?

Cradle-to-gate for physical goods. That includes raw material extraction, processing, manufacturing, and inbound logistics to our warehouse. It does not include outbound freight (we report that separately per shipment) or use-phase energy (where it varies wildly by customer infrastructure).

2. Who audits it?

Sphera (formerly thinkstep) is our LCA practitioner. They re-review the model annually and re-certify every SKU whose bill of materials changes by >5%. The audit report is available under NDA to enterprise customers.

3. What standards?

ISO 14067 for the carbon footprint of products. ISO 14064-1 for our organizational Scope 1, 2, and 3 inventory. GHG Protocol for the underlying methodology. EPD International framework where the product is in a sector with an existing PCR (product category rules).

4. How current is it?

The per-SKU table is refreshed quarterly. The data on any product page is timestamped. If you downloaded our numbers six months ago, they're already stale — the CSV link above always points at the current quarter.

Software

CO2e for inference, the same way we report it for chairs.

Our AI/ML software SKUs return CO2e in grams per API call, in the response header, on every request. The same number appears on the product detail page, on the sustainability page, and in the monthly usage report.

Region

Frankfurt (default) and Stockholm, both on 100% contracted renewable electricity via long-term power purchase agreements (PPAs) with verified European wind + solar operators.

Per-call measurement

Response header X-Officraft-CO2e-grams. Computed from GPU-seconds × datacenter PUE × grid emission factor (0, for our renewable-PPA regions). Audited by Sphera.

Data residency + retention

Zero-retention inference by default. Customer prompts and completions are not stored, logged, or used for training. Customer-owned keys are encrypted at rest with HSM-managed KMS.

Compliance

SOC 2 Type II + ISO 27001 certified. EU AI Act Article 53 GPAI obligations met. GDPR Art. 28 DPA included by default. The DPA covers EU and UK data residency commitments.
