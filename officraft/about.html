About Officraft

A company that writes things down.

We started Officraft because we had spent ten years buying office equipment for engineering companies and we were tired of the seam between "office" and "IT." Our first customers were the same people we used to buy for. They've stayed.

The founding story

Why we built this.

Officraft is a German GmbH founded in 2023 by Lena Voss, Tomás Ribeiro, and a small group of engineers and procurement specialists. We had all, separately, spent the better part of a decade trying to kit out offices for engineering teams — and we had all separately arrived at the same conclusion: the procurement workflow for an office is broken because the catalogs are broken. Office suppliers sell pens and don't sell servers. IT suppliers sell servers and don't sell pens. Nobody sells both.

The closer we looked, the more seams we found. The sustainability data is on a corporate brochure but not on the product page. The procurement integration is "and many more" but not actually SAP Ariba. The account manager has a name on the website but no working phone number. The catalog has a "sustainability" page but no per-SKU CO2e. We built Officraft to remove the seams.

In 2025 we added EU-hosted AI/ML software to the catalog — foundation models, fine-tuning, RAG, document AI, speech, vision, MLOps. The procurement teams we serve are increasingly bundling their AI infrastructure spend with their office infrastructure spend, because both roll up to the same person. So we made them the same cart, with the same account manager, the same net-30 terms, and the same per-SKU CO2e.

We are 38 people. We are not for sale. We are ISO 14001 and ISO 9001 certified, ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II certified for the software stack, and we publish our supply chain on this page below.
