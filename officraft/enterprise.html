§ 02 — Mechanics, in detail

Nine things that make a procurement manager's job shorter.

Every mechanic below is verified on a real customer account before we ship the feature. The diagrams are the spec sheets, not marketing abstractions.

01 / 09

Single unified cart

Office supplies, IT hardware, meeting-room AV, network infrastructure, AI/ML software — one cart, one freight quote, one PO, one invoice. Your existing ERP gets one line item per PO, with the SKU breakdown attached.

02 / 09

Bulk pricing visible per SKU

Four tiers per SKU — 1–9, 10–49, 50–499, 1,000+ — visible on every product page without login. Software uses the same ladder shape with token / page / seat / hour units.

03 / 09

Net-30 / Net-60 terms

Net-30 from 5,000 EUR aggregate order value. Net-60 from 25,000 EUR. Net-90 available for strategic accounts on request. Standard PO format attached to every shipment.

04 / 09

Per-SKU CO2e + Scope 3

Every physical SKU has cradle-to-gate CO2e, verified by Sphera. Every software SKU has per-call CO2e in the API response header. Both feed directly into your Scope 3 disclosure without a sales-rep email.

05 / 09

Dedicated account manager

One named human with a phone number, attached to orders above 5,000 EUR. They handle quoting, custom branding, delivery escalation, and reverse logistics. Average response time under 4 hours, business hours.

06 / 09

Single freight quote, cross-category

Whether your order is 200 chairs + 4 servers + 50km of cabling + a 1B-token LLM commitment, you get one freight line item at checkout. The cross-category mechanic no incumbent offers.

07 / 09

Punchout / cXML / OCI

SAP Ariba, Coupa, Oracle, Jaggaer, Dynamics 365, Sage Intacct, Workday, Odoo — supported with the real cXML punchout spec, not a marketing-page claim. Your users stay in your procurement portal; pricing reflects their account.

08 / 09

Custom branding at scale

For orders above 5,000 units, custom branding is included: your logo on the pen barrel, your colors on the cable ties, your company name in the room-display screensaver. No setup fee.

09 / 09

Free take-back at end of life

Chairs, desks, displays, servers, conference bars. We handle the logistics. You receive a diversion certificate for your Scope 3 report. Currently operational in 14 EU countries.
